Let the cabinetry continue beyond the kitchen.

Maryani extends the same planning, materials, proportions, and fitted detail into pantry, dining, living, entry, and other connected spaces.

Discuss connected cabinetry

A pantry, dining cabinet, media wall, entry unit, or living-room built-in can either strengthen the kitchen or make the main floor feel assembled from different catalogues. Connected millwork gives those rooms one material and architectural conversation.

01

Decide what should continue.

Some elements should repeat—material, datum, profile, or reveal—while others should shift so each room keeps its own purpose.

02

Design the storage around the room.

Serving pieces, pantry goods, coats, books, media, display, and household equipment need different depths, openings, and access.

03

Resolve junctions and installation.

Floors, walls, ceilings, trim, power, lighting, vents, and existing architecture determine how fitted work meets the home.

One home, not one repeated product

Show us where the kitchen should visually or functionally continue.

We can map the connected cabinetry as part of the kitchen or as a focused millwork scope for the surrounding rooms.

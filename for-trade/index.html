A clear cabinetry route for a professionally led project.

Mariani can support defined kitchen, cabinetry, and connected millwork scopes where the design authority, documentation, responsibilities, approvals, site conditions, and installation path are clear.

Submit a trade project

Trade work needs a different conversation from a homeowner project. The useful starting point is the current design stage, drawing set, decision authority, cabinetry scope, site and appliance conditions, required services, installation responsibility, and timing dependencies.

01

Share the current design authority.

Identify who owns the room design, client decisions, revisions, appliance coordination, finish approval, and final sign-off.

02

Define the cabinetry package.

Provide the relevant drawings, elevations, schedules, materials, interior requirements, integration details, site information, and required deliverables.

03

Make handoffs explicit.

Measurement, production release, delivery, protection, installation, adjustment, trade coordination, and changes need clear owners before work begins.

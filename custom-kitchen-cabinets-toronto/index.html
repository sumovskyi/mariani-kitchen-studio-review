Custom Kitchen Cabinets · Toronto & GTA

Cabinetry made for the room—and for everything that happens inside it.

Mariani designs the elevations and the interiors together, then connects custom production and installation to the conditions of the home.

Plan the cabinetry

Good cabinetry is more than the fronts. It is the reach of a drawer, the weight carried by a runner, the alignment of a long elevation, the clearance around an appliance, and the way the finished work meets the walls, floor, light, and architecture.

01

Plan the outside and inside together.

The visual rhythm, door sizes, appliance panels, shelves, drawers, pantry storage, waste, and task-specific interiors should all come from the same use plan.

02

Design for the actual opening.

Handles, integrated profiles, hinges, drawer travel, lift-up fronts, and pocket openings change grip, cleaning, clearance, movement, and appearance.

03

Measure and fit for the home.

Production drawings and site measure keep the cabinetry connected to real walls, floors, services, appliances, and the final installation condition.

Inside matters

Start with what the cabinetry needs to hold, hide, reveal, and support.

Tell us what is not working now and which parts of the room are staying. We can begin with the cabinetry scope before every finish is known.

Mariani Kitchen Studio · Toronto & GTA

A kitchen you love living in, with every dollar working where it matters.

Mariani plans the whole room around how you want it to look and work, then keeps the approved direction connected through materials, custom production, installation and any agreed renovation. It is Capable Group's dedicated kitchen studio, backed by its team and 25+ years in business.

Made for life around it

A well-planned kitchen makes the whole home easier to live in.

You feel it before you notice the details. Two people can move without working around each other, the things used every day have a natural place, and guests can stay close without standing in the work zone. The room feels calm because the hard decisions were made before it was built.

Mariani was created to give the kitchen the attention of a dedicated studio without separating it from the renovation experience behind the work.

The same team keeps the room, cabinetry, installation and any agreed work around it connected—especially when the right kitchen also depends on light, flooring, services or an adjoining room.

Clear from the start

Know what the kitchen includes before you compare the price.

Some kitchens need new cabinetry and installation. Others only work when lighting, plumbing, flooring, walls or the adjoining main floor change with them. You should not have to work that out alone. Mariani studies the room with you, then defines what can stay, what must change and who is responsible—so the design, scope and quote all describe the same job.

From custom cabinetry to a complete kitchen-connected renovation, we recommend the right scope after we understand the room.

Spend where it changes the room

Put the budget where you will see and use the difference.

Custom work earns its cost when it improves the layout, storage, working surfaces, fit or connection to the rest of the home. We explain what each important choice changes—and where a simpler option will do the same job.

A lower quote may simply describe a smaller project. Prices are comparable only when the drawings, materials, installation, allowances, exclusions and responsibility describe the same job.

Materials & Technology

What you see, touch, open and clean should work together.

Great materials are not chosen one sample at a time. A front changes beside the worktop and floor. A handle changes the line of the cabinetry and the feel of every opening. An interior earns its place when it brings the things you use into reach. We compare the whole combination—in the room's light and against the way you will live with it—before it is ordered.
